East Africa

From the Ethiopian highlands to the Swahili coast and the hills of Rwanda.

Food culture

East Africa includes Ethiopia, Eritrea, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and Burundi. Food here varies widely: the Ethiopian highlands are famous for teff and fiery berbere spice, while the Swahili coast shows centuries of trade with Arabia and India in its pilau, coconut curries and chapati.

In the Great Lakes region, including Rwanda, meals are simple and nourishing, built around beans, plantains, sweet potatoes and leafy greens. Food is shared with family and neighbors, and coffee, which first came from Ethiopia, is served with its own ceremony.

Staple foods

  • Teff
  • Maize (ugali)
  • Plantain (matoke)
  • Beans
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Cassava
  • Sorghum
  • Leafy greens

Cooking techniques

  • Teff batter is fermented for days to make sour, spongy injera.
  • Stews (wot) start with onions cooked slowly without oil, then spiced with berbere and niter kibbeh, a spiced butter.
  • Meat is grilled over charcoal as nyama choma in Kenya and as brochettes in Rwanda.
